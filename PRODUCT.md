# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Pregnant women and new mothers in Karanganyar and surrounding Central Java, Indonesia. Likely referred by friends or searching for a trusted alternative to large public hospitals. They browse on mobile, often WhatsApp-first in decision-making. They want to feel safe and cared for before they even walk in. A first-timer researching birth options; a returning mother who wants continuity of care.

## Product Purpose

Motherlight Birth Center is a maternal and neonatal clinic offering OB/GYN specialist care, baby spa, physiotherapy, pharmacy, and inpatient/outpatient services. The website's job is to build enough trust that a woman calls or messages to book an appointment. Secondary: communicate the warmth and professionalism that distinguishes Motherlight from generic hospitals.

## Brand Personality

Nurturing, trusted, serene. Warm without being saccharine. Professional without clinical coldness. A place where Indonesian mothers feel held.

## Anti-references

Generic hospital blue-and-white sterility. Western healthcare "clean slate" minimal design. Pastel pink babyware aesthetics. Generic SaaS landing page structure dropped onto a medical brief.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth earns trust** — every visual choice should make a visitor feel welcomed before they read a word.
2. **Clarity over decoration** — information hierarchy serves a mother who may be anxious or in a hurry. Never bury the phone number.
3. **Indonesian context first** — WhatsApp is the primary channel; it should be the most prominent CTA. Content in Bahasa Indonesia, not translated English copy.
4. **Quiet confidence** — Motherlight doesn't need to shout. Restrained use of the terracotta accent, generous whitespace, and serif headings project calm authority.
5. **Consistency of care** — the same visual warmth across every page, not just the homepage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion respected. Sufficient contrast on all text. Touch targets 44px minimum on mobile.
